Privacy Policy

Wheel Cricket · Developed by PlayGame Team

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PlayGame Team ("we", "us" or "our") develops and operates Wheel Cricket. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Wheel Cricket, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

1. Gameplay: Online and Offline

Wheel Cricket supports offline and online gameplay. You can enjoy supported game modes without signing in or connecting to the internet. Online features, such as connecting with friends and participating in groups, require an internet connection and may require sign-in.

  • Offline play: Offline matches are played on your device. Online account features are not available while you are offline. Any game information retained locally is subject to your device and app settings.
  • Online play: When you use Play Online and its social features, the account and gameplay information described below is processed to provide those features.

2. Information We Collect or Store

When you use Play Online, we store the following information:

  • Username: Your account username.
  • Display name: The name shown to other players.
  • Friends: Your friend connections within the game.
  • Groups: Groups you create or join and the related membership information needed to operate those groups.
  • Match history: Records of your online matches, to support gameplay history and related online features.
  • Sign-in information: Authentication is handled using Google Sign-In and/or Firebase authentication services. We use these services to authenticate your account.

We do not need you to provide the contents of your personal contacts to use the friends feature; friend connections are maintained within the game. (If the app later requests access to device contacts, this statement must be updated before that feature is released.)

3. How We Use Information

We use the online information described in this policy to:

  • Create and authenticate your Wheel Cricket account.
  • Display your username and display name to other players where relevant.
  • Let you add and manage friends and participate in groups.
  • Provide online matches and maintain your match history.
  • Operate, maintain and protect the online features of the game.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Wheel Cricket's online data is stored using cloud infrastructure in the following locations:

  • Google Cloud: India.
  • Neon database: Singapore.

Depending on the feature and system involved, information may be processed or stored in either location. By using online features, you understand that your information may be processed in these locations.

5. Third-Party Services

Wheel Cricket uses Google Sign-In and/or Firebase for account authentication. These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's own privacy terms and policies. Online data is hosted using Google Cloud and Neon infrastructure providers. These providers process data as needed to provide their services.

You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and Neon's privacy information at Neon Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your online account information, including your username, display name, friends, groups and match history, until you delete your account. Information may remain in service-provider backups for a limited period according to the providers' backup and retention processes.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Wheel Cricket account and the associated online information. To request deletion:

  1. Visit our Wheel Cricket Account Deletion page and follow the instructions provided there; or
  2. Email akashk.puttur.93.main@gmail.com with an account deletion request. Include your Wheel Cricket username so that we can identify the account. Do not send your password.

Once your request is verified and processed, we will delete the account information covered by this policy from our active systems, subject to any retention required by applicable law or legitimate security requirements. Deleting the app from your device does not, by itself, delete your online account or its server-stored information.

Deleting your online account does not automatically remove locally stored app data from your device. You may need to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove local data.

8. Data Security

We use cloud and authentication services to operate the online features of Wheel Cricket. We take reasonable steps to protect account and gameplay information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Wheel Cricket is a general cricket game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through an online account in a way that should not have occurred, please contact PlayGame Team through the support contact on our Google Play Store listing so we can review the request.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its online features or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
